\section*{Acknowledgements}
This research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors.

\section*{Data availability}
All datasets used in this study are publicly available: the PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Database (EEGMMIDB) and the BCI Competition~IV Dataset~2a. Code, configurations, trained checkpoints, and per-fold outputs
are released at \url{https://github.com/lifeinpassion/eeg-lejepa}.

\section*{Author contributions}
\textbf{Ping Liang:} Software, Investigation, Formal Analysis, Data Curation, Visualization, Writing -- Original Draft Preparation. \textbf{Anton Louise P.
De Ocampo:} Conceptualization, Methodology, Supervision, Project Administration, Writing -- Review and Editing. All authors have read and
agreed to the published version of the manuscript.

\section*{Conflict of interest}
The authors declare no conflicts of interest.

\section*{Ethics statement}
The research adhered to all applicable laws and ethics standards. This study used only publicly available, de-identified datasets (the PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Database and the BCI Competition~IV Dataset~2a); no new data were collected from human participants, and therefore no ethics committee approval or informed consent was required.
